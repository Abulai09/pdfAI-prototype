Причина установлена по `/Producer`: оригиналы выпущены **`PDFsharp 1.50 / MigraDoc`** (а не iTextSharp, как для Kaspi), а PDFsharp сжимает не через zlib, а через собственную копию SharpZipLib — самостоятельную реализацию DEFLATE. Отличия от zlib не косметические: своё построение дерева Хаффмана (куча с упаковкой `freq<<8 | depth`, свой порядок слияния и своя раздача длин), отсутствие адаптивного досрочного разрыва блока (zlib каждые ~8K символов решает, не пора ли закрыть блок; здесь блок закрывается строго по заполнению буфера в 16384 символа), `COMPR_FUNC[4] = FAST` против `deflate_slow` у zlib, окно с индекса 1 вместо нуля.

Добавлен модуль `pdfsharp_deflate.py` — порт `DeflaterEngine` + `DeflaterHuffman` + `DeflaterPending` из SharpZipLib. **Корректность здесь не «похоже», а тождество: порт обязан побайтово воспроизвести все 56 оригинальных потоков корпуса из их же распакованного содержимого — 56/56, включая FontFile2 по 94 КБ.** Это и есть оракул; ничего слабее для такой задачи не годится.

Отладка заняла ровно две итерации, и обе точки поучительны. Первая версия дала 0/56, но расходилась лишь с 4-го байта и была на 3-12 байт КОРОЧЕ оригинала. Сравнение потоков токенов показало, что разбор LZ77 совпадает **полностью** (803 токена в 803), то есть дело было целиком в Хаффмане. Виновник — `for (int i = 18; i > blTreeCodes; i--)`, где счётчик правится ПРЯМО В УСЛОВИИ: после первого же попадания условие становится ложным и цикл встаёт, тогда как питоновский `range(18, 4, -1)` вычисляется один раз и продолжает крутиться, перезаписывая значение более низким. Из-за этого HCLEN получался короче. Вторая — `prev[strstart & WMASK] = match = head[hash]` в C# присваивает СТАРУЮ голову цепочки, а не `strstart`.

Ради скорости развёрнутые вручную сравнения по 8 байт в `FindLongestMatch` заменены на подсчёт длины общего префикса разом (XOR двух срезов): цепочка `switch((scanMax-scan)%8)` + цикл по 8 подобраны в оригинале так, что проверка `scan == scanMax` попадает ровно на границу, поэтому результат тождественно равен «общему префиксу, ограниченному scanMax» — что и подтверждено оракулом. Побайтовый цикл на Python сделал бы один поток в 70 КБ неприемлемо медленным. **Пункт 2 — порядок записей ToUnicode ≠ порядку вёрстки. Поэтому правка не «вставлять в правильное место», а `_reorder_tounicode_to_text` — отдельный проход, приводящий порядок к фактическому порядку первого появления CID в ГОТОВЫХ байтах. ## Исправлено 2026-08-04: шесть дефектов Kaspi ИП (правый край «Дебет», профиль округления, повторы, размер файла)

Разбор шести пунктов ниже (они были планом на 2026-08-04, замеры сделаны на `C:\Users\Abylay\Desktop\testpdf\kaspiPay` — 4 реальных файла) показал **три** корневых причины, а не шесть: пункты 2/3/5 — одна политика округления, пункт 6 — следствие 2/3/5, а не самостоятельный дефект.

**Причина 1 (пункт 1): ширина цифры была константой-приближением, причём дважды.** `process_kaspi_ip_pdf` держит право-выровненную колонку «Дебет» тем, что при смене числа сдвигает X ровно на изменение ширины строки. Ширина считалась как `avg_w = 4.44` × «взвешенная длина» (пробел/запятая = 0.5 цифры). Оба множителя неточны: у ArialMT цифра = `_ARIAL_DIGIT_EM` × кегль = **4.448** pt при кегле 8 (весь формат набран восьмым), а пробел и запятая в /W этого шрифта = **277**, а не 278, то есть не ровно половина цифры. Первая ошибка давала дрейф `0.008 × (длина_новая − длина_старая)`, вторая — `0.008 × (разделителей_старых − разделителей_новых)`. Замер настоящими метриками шрифта (spans PyMuPDF, а не той же моделью, которой считает писатель — иначе проверка согласилась бы с его ошибкой по построению): в оригиналах **все** суммы «Дебет» стоят на одном крае (IP2 — 1686 сумм на 610.15 в координатах повёрнутой страницы, разброс нулевой), в результатах края расползались на 610.13/610.14/610.16. Исправлено переходом на реальные ширины: `_primary_glyph_advances()` читает /W дескендант-шрифта (того же, что даёт `build_dynamic_cmap` основным) и строит `char → доля em`; писатель считает ширину строки по ним. Константа `_ARIAL_DIGIT_EM` осталась только запасным путём, если /W не разобрался. После правки — 0 сбитых сумм на всех 4 файлах на всех достижимых целях.

**Причина 2 (пункты 2, 3, 5): `_round_amount` навязывала сетку в 1000 ₸ независимо от того, была ли круглой сама исходная сумма.** У IP2/IP3 (процессинговые счета) кратны 1000 всего 6.0% и 5.8% сумм кредита — настоящие суммы там вида `49 676`, `1 748 417`, `35,15`; безусловное округление давало 99.8% круглых, то есть распределение, ПРОТИВОПОЛОЖНОЕ оригинальному. Отдельно вредил пол `return rounded if rounded > 0 else unit`: любая сумма меньше половины шага выталкивалась ровно в 1 000 ₸, из-за чего на IP2 значение «1 000,00» встречалось 304 раза подряд в колонке дебета (в оригинале такой серии нет), а комиссия в 35 ₸ превращалась в тысячу. Исправлено наследованием профиля оригинала — решение принимается по КАЖДОЙ сумме, а не по формату (та же конвенция, что и «per-statement, не по хардкод-метке» в `recalculate_halyk`): если оригинал кратен крупному «человеческому» числу, шаг эскалируется к нему (как и раньше); если не кратен даже тысяче — результат не округляется до тысяч вовсе, а копейки наследуются от оригинала (`original % 1`); если круглая сумма при занижении провалилась ниже своего же укрупнённого шага — шаг возвращается к базовой тысяче, а не к копейкам. **Тот же пол вдобавок ломал занижение, и это оказалось хуже всего остального.** `return rounded if rounded > 0 else unit` возвращал не тысячу, а ЭСКАЛИРОВАННЫЙ шаг: у оригинала 50 000 ₸ шаг равен 50 000, поэтому занижённые до 23 253 ₸ округлялись к нулю и строка получала обратно **полные 50 000 ₸** — то есть занижения не происходило вовсе, а для оригинала в 1 000 000 ₸ так же «восстанавливался» миллион. Цель ×0.6 у этого файла остаётся **пограничной и зависящей от розыгрыша шума** — это свойство данных, а не регресс.

Наследование копеек — не косметика: ячейка печатается в форме СВОЕГО оригинала (`_format_amount_cell`, `had_decimal`), поэтому дробный результат в ячейке без копеек молча терял дробную часть при печати, тогда как шапка считалась по неокруглённым числам. ## Исправлено 2026-08-03: помесячное выравнивание убивало естественный разброс дохода (Kaspi Gold)

`recalculate_statement` (`pdf_service.py`) раньше считал `K_month = target_monthly_income / доход_этого_месяца` **отдельно для каждого месяца** — по построению это гарантированно выравнивало любой месяц ровно к `target_monthly_income` (`K_month × доход_месяца ≡ target`). На реальном файле это давало почти одинаковую сумму каждый месяц (пример из бага: май/июнь/июль/август — 2 557 000 / 2 573 000 / 2 551 000 / 2 565 000 ₸, при исходном разбросе 1 240 000 / 657 000 / 4 233 000 / 35 000 ₸) — визуальный tell того же класса, что и остальные пункты критерия 3 «Круглые/реалистичные цифры»: ни одна настоящая зарплата/оборот не даёт из месяца в месяц одну и ту же сумму, где-то густо, где-то пусто.

Исправлено переходом на **единый K на весь период**, применяемый равномерно ко всем месяцам (`month_K[mk] = global_K` вместо `target/доход_месяца`) — пропорции между месяцами остаются теми же, что и в оригинале, только масштабированные к целевому среднему. `global_K` был и остаётся клипнут к минимуму 1.0 (защита от занижения при апскейле), так что при равномерном применении ни один месяц не может уменьшиться относительно оригинала — старая помесячная защита `_clip_k_to_one` стала не нужна и удалена.

**ISI при этом становится инвариантом относительно множителя цели.** `main.py`'s `isi = max(0, 1 - sigma/mu)` — это `1 − коэффициент вариации` помесячных сумм; равномерное умножение всех месяцев на одну константу `c` не меняет `sigma/mu` (оба масштабируются на `c`), поэтому ISI обработанного файла **всегда равен ISI необработанного оригинала**, независимо от таргета. Это ожидаемо и корректно: ISI — мягкий сигнал в Kaspi Gold (не блокирует `passed`, см. `main._verify_pdf`), и настоящая нестабильность реального дохода не должна и не может «улучшаться» простым домножением. `total_income`/`balance_end`/сертификатные KZT/USD/EUR-суммы не затронуты — правки только в помесячном распределении salary-транзакций, формулы итогов (`Шаг 3`, running balance, `HeaderCellOverflowError`) не менялись.

Тот же баг тем же способом исправлен и в `pdf_service_downscale.py:recalculate_statement_downscale` (занижение дохода) — идентичная замена `month_K[mk] = target/доход_месяца` → `month_K[mk] = global_K`.

Проверено на реальном файле (`gold_statement (3).pdf`, тот же файл, что и в остальной форензике этого документа — 2025 транзакций, ISI оригинала 0.1217) через `tests/scripts/verify_gold_file.py --targets 1.05,2,5`: математика OK и позиции OK на всех трёх целях, коррекция «Шаг 3» ни разу не сработала даже на ×5 (равномерного масштабирования хватило, чтобы баланс не уходил в минус ни на одной границе дня), и `main._verify_pdf` подтвердил инвариантность ISI по факту: 0.1217 (оригинал) / 0.1277 (×2) / 0.1138 (×5) — колебания в пределах ±3%-шума на транзакцию, без тренда по множителю.

Halyk (`halyk_pdf_service.py:recalculate_halyk`) устроен так же (чистый `K = target / доход_месяца` без коридора) — не тронут в рамках этого исправления, не исследован отдельно на реальных файлах. Kaspi ИП (`kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py:recalculate_kaspi_ip`) уже решает ту же проблему иначе — «коридором» ±`_MAX_MONTH_K_SPREAD=3.5` вокруг единого K, — потому что там ISI является ЖЁСТКОЙ проверкой (`validate_kaspi_ip` добавляет её в `issues`, порог 0.60): полностью равномерный K на реальном файле там давал ISI=0.1987, ниже порога, поэтому чистый перенос этого исправления в Kaspi ИП без изменений сломает `passed` — не делать без отдельного анализа.

## Исправлено 2026-08-03 (там же): `_round_to_natural` не сохраняла круглость ПОСЛЕ умножения на дробный K (Kaspi Gold)

Побочный эффект правки выше — единый K сохраняет разброс между месяцами, а значит мелкие «Пополнение»-переводы (p2p-переводы от третьих лиц, не зарплата) больше не раздуваются искусственно до уровня среднего дохода и остаются мелкими, как в оригинале. На реальном файле это обнажило существовавший и раньше изъян `_round_to_natural`: шаг округления (50/100/500/1000) выбирался только по величине ИТОГОВОГО числа, а не по тому, насколько круглым было число ДО умножения. Круглый оригинал × дробный коэффициент (напр. K≈2.33) даёт число, которое формально попадает в шаг по своей величине, но не похоже на настоящий перевод: сматчено по (дата, отправитель) на реальном файле — `15 000 ₸ → 34 500 ₸`, `20 000 ₸ → 47 400 ₸`, `3 000 ₸ → 7 150 ₸`, `1 000 ₸ → 2 300 ₸`, `10 500 ₸ → 24 600 ₸`. Формально это проходило существующую проверку `check_natural_rounding` (значение лежит на своей базовой сетке), но человеку видно, что это «вычислено», а не введено вручную — тот же класс проблемы, что и остальные пункты критерия 3, просто с другой стороны: раньше (до правки выше) мелкие переводы маскировались, раздуваясь в крупный диапазон с более грубым и потому визуально более «круглым» шагом.

Исправлено добавлением необязательного параметра `_round_to_natural(val, original=None)`: если исходная сумма `original` сама кратна более крупному «человеческому» числу (5 000/10 000/50 000/100 000/500 000/1 000 000 — `_NATURAL_STEP_CANDIDATES`, каждое кандидатное число — точный делитель следующего, поэтому шаг никогда не выходит за пределы уже существующей базовой сетки по величине), шаг округления результата подтягивается к ней, а не остаётся мелким шагом базового диапазона. Никогда не делает шаг МЕЛЬЧЕ базового — только крупнее, когда это подтверждено оригиналом. С правкой те же пары дают: `15 000 → 35 000`, `20 000 → 50 000`, `3 000 → 7 000`, `1 000 → 2 000`, `10 500 → 25 000` — неотличимо от того, что человек вручную перевёл круглую сумму.

Применено во всех точках, где `_round_to_natural` вызывается на зарплатную транзакцию в `pdf_service.py` (основной цикл масштабирования И «Шаг 3» — в «Шаге 3» передаётся именно `tx.amount`, истинный оригинал, а не уже промасштабированный `tx.new_amount` с прошлой итерации, иначе шаг «сползал бы» с реальной круглости на каждой итерации ×1.05) и `pdf_service_downscale.py` (основной цикл и post-check ×1.02-коррекция). Не тронуто в `halyk_pdf_service.py._realistic_round` — используется другая, отдельная функция, не исследовано.

Проверено на реальном файле: `check_natural_rounding` (уже существующая проверка «лежит ли значение на СВОЕЙ базовой сетке по величине») по-прежнему OK на всех целях — это гарантировано математически (каждый кандидатный шаг кратен предыдущему, более мелкому), не только эмпирически. `pytest tests/` — 43 passed / 69 skipped, без изменений.

## 2026-08-03: постоянные проверки в `verify_*_file.py`/`verify_any_file.py` для критериев выше

Изначально фиксы выше (единый K + эскалация шага округления, для всех трёх личных форматов) были проверены только вручную, разовыми скриптами — а существующий `check_natural_rounding` этого не ловит вообще: старый баг (`K_month = target/доход_месяца`) и фикс (единый K) одинаково лежат на «своей» базовой сетке по величине, разница только в ТОМ, какой именно шаг выбран, а это не то же самое, что «лежит на сетке». pytest tests/test_serialization_style.py                                        # no fixtures needed

`tests/fixtures/` is not present in every checkout of this repo — it holds real, unmodified bank PDFs and is deliberately gitignored (`.gitignore`: `tests/fixtures/`, "Реальные PDF-выписки для тестов: никогда не коммитим"). **A missing fixture is a SKIP, not a failure** — `tests/conftest.py` turns "file not found under `tests/fixtures/`" into `pytest.skip` with the reason spelled out, so a red run means something is actually broken and nothing else. On a fixtures-less checkout the suite is **71 passed / 69 skipped / 0 failed** (was 43 passed before 2026-08-04's two fixture-free test files — `test_kaspi_ip_rounding_policy.py` +17, `test_kaspi_ip_coordinate_precision.py` +11); supply fixtures for the three per-format files below (`test_pdf_service.py`, `test_halyk_pdf_service.py`, `test_kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py`) and the skips turn into real runs. Do not re-introduce the old habit of diffing the `FAILED` list against a memorized baseline — there is no baseline of failures any more.

The hook deliberately catches **two** exception types: `path.read_bytes()` raises the builtin `FileNotFoundError`, while `fitz.open(path)` raises `pymupdf.FileNotFoundError`, which subclasses `RuntimeError` (**not** the builtin) and leaves `.filename` empty — its path is only in the message text. Both spellings occur across the three per-format test files. Anything outside `tests/fixtures/` still fails normally, so a production code path that loses its own file is not silently skipped.

`tests/test_halyk_pdf_service.py` and `tests/test_kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py` round-trip real PDF fixtures (`tests/fixtures/halyk_nav_*.pdf`, `tests/fixtures/kaspi_ip_*.pdf`) through parse → recalculate → `process_*_pdf` (raw byte rewrite) → `validate_*`, at downscale/upscale/near-no-op targets, plus a few focused unit tests per module (Y-row-grouping boundary and the inline-`Tf`-between-`Td`-and-`Tj` header quirk for Halyk; running-balance-never-negative-on-upscale for Kaspi ИП, since scaleable debit there — unlike Halyk/Kaspi Gold — grows with income). The fixtures are frozen snapshots — if they are ever replaced with newly generated files, rerun `python tests/scripts/print_fixture_targets.py` to recompute floor-safe `target_monthly_income` values for each `ROUNDTRIP_CASES` list, since each generated statement has different random amounts and therefore a different downscale floor (`print_fixture_targets.py` does not yet cover `kaspi_gold_cert_*.pdf` — recompute targets for those manually via `main._verify_pdf(raw)["summary"]["avg_monthly_income"]` if those fixtures are replaced).

`tests/test_pdf_service.py` covers Kaspi Gold (cert-format only — all 5 fixtures have a "Справка об остатке" page 0) via the same round-trip principle, using real fixtures `tests/fixtures/kaspi_gold_cert_original.pdf` (2333 tx/13mo, active card, has `Поступление`/`Зачисление` self-transfer rows), `kaspi_gold_cert_scored.pdf` (same card after one prior `/process` run — floor ends up _below_ its now-inflated average, unlike original/small/original2, so scored/scored2 are the only ones exercising a successful downscale), `kaspi_gold_cert_small.pdf` (157 tx/2mo, also has self-transfer rows), and `kaspi_gold_cert_original2.pdf`/`kaspi_gold_cert_scored2.pdf` (3581 tx/13mo, a different real card with _zero_ self-transfer rows — added specifically to confirm the self-transfer fixes below don't regress statements that never hit that code path; see `FIXTURES_WITH_SELF_TRANSFER` in the test file for which fixtures exercise which checks). It also pins two Kaspi-Gold-specific fixes found via the original/small fixtures: (1) `parse_full_statement` didn't recognize `Поступление`/`Зачисление` (self-transfer from a Kaspi Deposit sub-account) as transaction types at all — real rows worth up to ~19M ₸ silently vanished from `stmt.transactions`, breaking the balance-identity check even on an untouched original; fixed via `TX_TYPES_SELF_TRANSFER`, classified `is_refund=True` (so it's included in balance math but never scaled as salary, same treatment as a purchase refund). (2) Fixing (1) then exposed a second bug in `process_pdf_bytes_raw`'s raw-byte writer: its own row-type detection (`y_has_refund_type`, matching type-words as `Td`/`Tj` tokens in the _same_ content stream as the amount) never finds anything on real Kaspi Gold PDFs — the type/description text isn't among that stream's Tj tokens at all — so a self-transfer's `REFUND_IDENTITY` queue slot (meant to be a same-value no-op so it doesn't steal a salary replacement) never got consumed, permanently blocking the front of that value's FIFO queue and shifting every later same-valued cell onto the wrong transaction. Fixed with `page_refund_cs_ys`, a per-page Y-position filter mirroring the one already used for salary cells (built from `page.get_text("words")`, not the raw content stream, so it works regardless of stream layout). Also found and fixed while adding this suite: `_collect_amount_on_line`'s transaction-parsing call used a hardcoded `x_min=120.0`, calibrated for ≤6-digit amounts — a 7-8 digit amount's leading digit can legitimately render left of x=120, so it was silently dropped (`1 899 000,00` parsed as `899 000,00`); replaced with a dynamic `x_min` computed from that row's own date-token right edge. And: `recalculate_statement` could compute a negative `new_balance_end` after its own running-balance correction step ("Шаг 3") and still let `process_pdf_bytes_raw` write it — but Kaspi Gold's "Доступно на …" header often has no sign glyph in the text at all, and a `-` glyph can't reliably be synthesized (its CID exists only in a non-primary font's CMap, not the one `build_dynamic_cmap`'s `from_unicode` is built from), so the written amount would look positive while `stmt.new_balance_end` was actually negative. Now raises `IncomeTooLowError(reason="post_check_negative_balance")` instead, matching the "solved architecturally, not by patching the cmap" approach `pdf_service_downscale.py`'s own docstring already commits to for its downscale floors.

Both validators' "Целостность стримов" check used to slice a stream's end by searching for the literal text `"endstream"` and guessing a 1-or-2-byte separator via `sd.endswith(b"\r\n"/b"\n")`. Since recompressed zlib output can coincidentally end in `0x0D`, that guess sometimes strips one real payload byte too many, producing a false-positive "N битых стримов" on an otherwise valid, correctly-processed PDF — this was caught by `tests/test_kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py::test_validate_suggested_min_is_achievable`. Both validators now slice by the declared `/Length` instead (the same fix `process_kaspi_ip_pdf`'s own write path already used, per its comment there). The Kaspi Gold path carries the same fix: `_verify_pdf` lives in `main.py` (not `pdf_service.py`) and delegates this check to `main.py:_check_stream_integrity`, which slices by `/Length` and documents the shared origin in its own docstring. All three formats are covered — if a new format adds its own stream-integrity check, slice by `/Length`, never by an `endswith(b"\r\n")` guess.

**Per-file autotest battery (the way a newly-supplied real PDF gets checked against all 4 quality criteria):**

```
python tests/scripts/verify_any_file.py <file.pdf> [...] [--targets 1.05,2,5] [--render]
python tests/scripts/verify_gold_file.py <file.pdf> [<file2.pdf> ...] [--targets 1.05,2,10] [--render]
python tests/scripts/verify_halyk_file.py <file.pdf> [...]      # same CLI
python tests/scripts/verify_kaspi_ip_file.py <file.pdf> [...]   # same CLI
python tests/scripts/print_fixture_targets.py                   # recompute floor-safe fixture targets
```

**`verify_any_file.py` is the universal one — prefer it, and it is fixture-free by design.** It takes _any_ document as a literal CLI path, detects the format itself with the same dispatcher `main.py:/process` uses (Halyk → Kaspi ИП → business cert → Kaspi Gold), and runs **every** criterion for that format in one report — no pre-registered list, works on a file it has never seen. It is also the only battery that covers **business certificates** at all. It reuses the check functions imported from the three profile scripts rather than reimplementing them, so "ran the universal one" and "ran the profile one" cannot diverge. On top of the per-format criteria it adds format-independent structural checks that neither math nor geometry sees: `is_repaired` (PyMuPDF silently repairing a structurally broken file — every other check then runs against the repaired copy and passes), page count unchanged, and **page-0 font set unchanged** (the check that caught the substitute-font defects in criterion 4). Exit code 1 on any FAIL; guards are reported as `guard`, not FAIL. A combined `verify_any_report.json` is written to the current directory; per-file outputs still land in `<stem>_autotest_out/` next to the input.

A pytest suite that scanned `tests/fixtures/` to apply every criterion under `pytest` was built and then deliberately deleted: it worked, but it necessarily operated on a fixed, named list of files copied into that directory — the opposite of "any document, including ones never seen before," which is what was actually asked for. `verify_any_file.py` is that deliverable. Do not recreate a fixtures-scanning pytest suite for this purpose; if pytest-integrated criteria coverage is wanted later, parametrize it explicitly over real paths supplied at invocation time, not over a directory listing.

Each script takes one or more paths to an **original, unprocessed** PDF of its format, runs it through the real production pipeline (the same upscale/downscale dispatch `main.py:/process` uses) at a battery of target multipliers of that file's own current average income, and checks math + geometry + rounding + serialization style on every result. Output PDFs and the report land next to the input, in a per-format directory: `<stem>_autotest_out/` (gold), `<stem>_halyk_autotest_out/` (Halyk), `<stem>_ip_autotest_out/` (Kaspi ИП). `--render` additionally dumps PNGs of pages 0/1. Exit code is 1 if any target FAILs, so these are CI-usable. `--targets` overrides that script's `DEFAULT_TARGET_MULTIPLIERS`, which is **not** the same list in every script: gold is `[0.6, 1.05, 2, 10, 50]`, Halyk and Kaspi ИП are `[0.6, 1.05, 2, 5, 20]`. A FAIL on the _original_ file (low ISI, negative balance) is a property of real data, not a regression — the scripts only judge the processed results.

**There is no linter or formatter in this project** — `requirements-dev.txt` is `-r requirements.txt` plus `pytest>=8.0.0` and nothing else. Don't add a "run the linter" step that doesn't exist; match the surrounding file's style by hand.

## Quality criteria — the 5 things that matter for ANY generated PDF

Every format (Kaspi Gold, Kaspi ИП, Halyk, business certs) is judged against these criteria, in order of how catastrophic a failure is. When adding a format, a target range, or a code path, verify against all of them — a fix for one has repeatedly turned out to regress or leave a hole in another during this project's audits.

1. **Математика/арифметика (math/arithmetic)** — every balance identity must close: `balance_start + Σ(+) − Σ(−) = balance_end` at the transaction level, the same via the header's own `total_income`/`total_expense` fields, and the running-balance chain must never go negative. This is checked by `main._verify_pdf()` (Kaspi Gold), `kaspi_ip_pdf_service.validate_kaspi_ip()`, `halyk_pdf_service.validate_halyk()` — the same functions `/verify` calls in production. **Running-balance non-negativity must be checked at DAY boundaries, not per-transaction** (see `pdf_service.min_dayend_balance`/`first_negative_dayend`, ported to Halyk as `_halyk_dayend_min_rb`) — none of these statement formats carry intra-day timestamps, so the order of same-date rows in the PDF is arbitrary, and a per-transaction check produces false-positive "overdraft" on a genuinely valid statement (caught for real on an unmodified `gold_statement.pdf`: −54.17 ₸ from 5 debits rendered before 2 same-day covering credits). ISI (income/turnover stability index) is a **soft** signal in Kaspi Gold (reported, doesn't block `passed`) but a **hard** one in Kaspi ИП/Halyk (`validate_kaspi_ip`/`validate_halyk` add it to `issues`) — know which regime you're in before assuming a low ISI on an unmodified original is a bug (it usually isn't — real income/turnover is naturally irregular). Also audit for arithmetic relationships that exist in the data model but aren't covered by any existing check before assuming "all checks pass" means "all math is right" — e.g. the cert-page USD/EUR proportionality to KZT (against the original implied exchange rate) had no check anywhere (production or test) until specifically audited by hand; it turned out correct, but that was verified, not assumed.

2. **Позиционирование цифр и шрифт (text positioning and font)** — no two words on the same visual row may overlap in X after a number grows/shrinks digits (checked programmatically via `find_line_overlaps()` in `tests/scripts/verify_gold_file.py`/`verify_kaspi_ip_file.py`/`verify_halyk_file.py` — same technique, one copy per format), AND font name/size must not change (Halyk's autotest additionally checks this; a bank-issued PDF re-rendering a number in a different font or a shrunk size is a tell). Programmatic geometry checks catch bounding-box overlap but not everything a human notices — **actually render pages to PNG and look at them** (`fitz` `get_pixmap(dpi=150)`, then view the file) at the most extreme target you're testing (largest digit-count growth is where this breaks), not just at the geometry-checker's pass/fail. A short original balance ("1 898,08") growing to 13+ digits after a large upscale was the real bug this class of check was built to catch — it passed the _math_ check while visually overflowing the table's left edge.

   **Word-vs-word overlap is not sufficient on its own — three blind spots were found on a real file (`gold_statement.pdf`, 42 pages, cert format) that `find_line_overlaps()` cannot see by construction, and `verify_gold_file.py` now covers all three:** (a) it was only ever called on pages 0 and 1 (`geometry_check(..., extra_pages=[])` — the argument was never filled), so the ~40 pages that actually carry the transaction table were never checked at all; (b) a table border is a _vector line_, not a word, so a number can cross its own cell's frame without colliding with any text — `find_frame_overflows()` now checks words against stroked (`s`/`fs`) cell rects, deliberately ignoring `f` fills since those are invisible and produce false positives; (c) the «Сумма» column is right-aligned, so a _uniform_ misalignment shifts every scaled amount together and never overlaps anything — `check_column_alignment()` now asserts the multiset of right edges (x1 of the `₸` glyph per row) is unchanged from the original. That last check is what exposed the real bug below. `check_fonts()` (font name/size multiset vs. original) and `check_natural_rounding()` (criterion 3) were also missing from the Kaspi Gold autotest and are now in it.

   **The X-shift after a number changes width must use the font's REAL digit advance.** `process_pdf_bytes_raw`'s hex writer had `avg_char_width = 4.0` hardcoded for statement pages (only the cert page measured its own width, into `cert_char_width`). ArialMT's digit is `_ARIAL_DIGIT_EM = 0.556` em — 5.28 pt at the body's 9.5 pt and 5.56 pt at the summary table's 10 pt — so 4.0 pt is ~76% of the truth and the left-shift kept undershooting the growth by ~24%, dragging each amount's right edge out of its column proportionally to how much it grew. Compounding it, `original_text` was `.strip()`ed while `new_text` was rebuilt from raw prefix/suffix and kept the cell's trailing spaces («+ 6 500,00 ₸ »), adding a constant phantom left-shift of ~3 spaces. On `gold_statement.pdf` the two errors partly cancelled in the body (right edges landed at 187.5/188.1/189.4 instead of the column's single 190.93 — a visibly ragged column, on every processed file, at every target) and reinforced each other in the header (closing balance crossed its cell's right border by 1.4 pt at ×2, 5.3 pt at ×200). Fixed via `_digit_width_at()`, which reads the actual kegel from the last `/Fx N Tf` before the match (`_ambient_tf`), plus `.strip()` on both sides of the length comparison. After the fix every output reproduces the original's exact edge set (190.93/270.26/300.76) at every target ×0.99…×200, and downscale (which shares this writer via `process_downscale`) is clean too.

   **Summary-table cell capacity above ~1 млрд ₸ — hard-refused, not silently written.** With the alignment correct, a 10-digit amount right-aligned at the column's anchor (x=300.76) starts at x≈205.4, which is 2.3 pt left of that cell's own left border (x=207.7); the cell has 5.5 pt of unused padding on the _right_, so the string fits but the right-aligned position does not. It first appears when the closing balance or total income reaches 10 digits (≈ ×75 of this file's average, i.e. a balance over 1 000 000 000 ₸ — a target that already fails criterion 3 on realism grounds either way). Confirmed on a batch of 13 real cert-format files (`tests/scripts/verify_gold_file.py` run against `testpdf/gold/*`): 9 of 13 hit it at ×50 (one, with an unusually high average income, already at ×10) — always the same two header fields (`total_income`/`balance_end`), always the same 2.31 pt overflow, since the cell's coordinates are a fixed template position shared by every cert-format file, not per-file. Clamping the position was considered and rejected (same reasoning as when this was first found): it would either require mapping the drawn cell rects into content-stream coordinates inside the writer and break the column's right-alignment invariant for that one row (`check_column_alignment` would then fail instead), or shrink the font for just that cell (`check_fonts` would then fail instead) — every fix-in-place trades one geometry class for another. Instead, `recalculate_statement` now raises `HeaderCellOverflowError` (`pdf_service.py`, checked right after the existing `new_balance_end < 0` guard, same architectural spot) whenever either field's magnitude would exceed `_HEADER_CELL_MAX_SAFE_VALUE = 999_999_999`, refusing _before_ the PDF is written — the same "solved architecturally, not by patching" precedent as `IncomeTooLowError`. `main.py:/process` catches it and returns HTTP 400 with `reason: "header_cell_overflow"` (does **not** populate `min_desired_income`, since there is no "minimum" here — it's a ceiling, not a floor — so the frontend's floor-specific "Подставить минимум?" auto-fill button correctly does _not_ fire for this error; it falls through to the generic error-message display). `verify_gold_file.py`'s battery now treats a caught `HeaderCellOverflowError` as a `cell-guard` row (same non-failing treatment as `floor-guard`/`IncomeTooLowError`), not a FAIL.

3. **Круглые/реалистичные цифры (round / realistic numbers)** — a scaled amount must look like a real transaction a bank would have recorded, not like `amount × K × (1±noise)` rounded to 2 decimals verbatim. This is _not_ "always round to whole tenge" — it's "match what THIS format's real, unmodified data actually looks like": Kaspi Gold salary deposits are always whole tenge in human steps (`pdf_service._round_to_natural`: steps of 50/100/500/1000 depending on magnitude); Kaspi ИП scaled amounts round to the nearest 1000 (`_round_amount`), deliberately deferred to a single pass at the very end of `recalculate_kaspi_ip` (rounding mid-loop breaks the balance-correction loop's convergence — small corrections get eaten by the 1000-unit step); Halyk is genuinely mixed — real _nav_-format (Russian-keyword) salaries/seizures are always whole tenge in the same stepped convention as Kaspi Gold, but real _legacy_-format (Kazakh-phrase) salaries genuinely DO carry cents (e.g. `299 374,05 ₸` on a real file) — so `recalculate_halyk()` decides **per-statement, from the statement's own real (unscaled) salary transactions**, not from a hardcoded format label (more robust to variants not yet seen): if none of them have cents, route scaled amounts through `_round_to_natural`; if they do, leave `round(x, 2)` as-is (already realistic there). **The specific bug class to watch for**: a _balance-correction boost loop_ (any code that iteratively multiplies an amount by ~1.02–1.05 to fix a negative running balance) is easy to write with a bare `round(x, 2)` since that's what "just fix the number" naturally reads as — but iterating a natural-looking amount through repeated multiplication reliably produces amounts with arbitrary cents. This exact bug was found and fixed in three places this way: `pdf_service.recalculate_statement`'s Шаг 3 (100 of 561 real salary transactions had random cents like `232 682,62 ₸` before the fix), `pdf_service_downscale.recalculate_statement_downscale`'s post-check boost, and would apply to any future correction loop added anywhere in this codebase — check every `round(x * factor, 2)` inside a retry/correction loop against this criterion specifically, they're the highest-risk spot for this class of bug.

4. **Стиль сериализации операторов (how the rewritten operators are WRITTEN)** — the rewritten content-stream tokens must be byte-indistinguishable in style from the ones the original generator wrote. This criterion exists because of a forensic comparison of 11 outputs against 3 originals (full report: `docs/forensics-2026-08-02.md`). Everything a reader normally checks was already clean — `/Producer`, `/Creator`, `/CreationDate`, `/ModDate`, trailer `/ID`, PDF header version, object count, fonts, page count all identical; no incremental update (one `%%EOF`); xref offsets and `/Length` correctly recomputed; no `/Encrypt`, no `/Sig`. Only ~20–29% of objects differed, exactly the ones holding amounts. What gave it away was the **handwriting** of the edited tokens, and the separation was total — zero occurrences in every original, 102–247 in every output, no intermediate values, count equal to the number of edited amounts:
   - **Признак 1 — избыточные нули в координатах.** The generator writes operands compactly (`42.5`, `211`, `219.31`); the writer emitted fixed 5-decimal (`42.50000`, `211.00000`). Fixed by `pdf_service._fmt_coord()` — same `:.5f` rounding, trailing zeros stripped, so the **value and therefore the geometry is unchanged** (`check_column_alignment` sees the same edges). `-0` is normalized to `0`.
   - **Признак 2 — Td и Tj склеены в одну строку.** The gold_statement format always puts them on separate lines; the writer joined them.
   - **Признак 3 — пробел перед Tj.** The Kaspi ИП format writes `)Tj` flush (1669 times, zero exceptions); the writer emitted `) Tj`.

   Признаки 2 and 3 are both fixed by `pdf_service._op_separators()`, which reads the separators **out of the original match** and reuses them, rather than hardcoding "newline for gold, flush for Выписка" — the rule "repeat whatever was there" is correct for both formats and for ones not yet seen. Halyk and Kaspi ИП import both helpers from `pdf_service`, same as `build_dynamic_cmap`/`_rebuild_xref_table`.

   `_fmt_coord`/`_op_separators` are unit-tested in `tests/test_serialization_style.py` (fixture-independent — runs in any checkout), and `style_check()` in all three `tests/scripts/verify_*_file.py` scripts (plus `verify_any_file.py`) pins the fingerprint counts against the original per run, folded into the "позиции"/"4 стиль" column. That check compares **in both directions, not "no worse than before"**: if a format ever turns up whose generator natively writes fixed-precision coordinates, `_fmt_coord`'s compact form would produce the same standout group of lines, only mirrored — such a file must fail loudly rather than ship. Verified end-to-end on the three real originals from the report: with the old writers `style_check` reports exactly the report's own numbers (163 redundant zeros / 247 glued lines on `gold_statement (3)` at ×2), with the fix all counts equal the original's at every achievable target. Also verified on 6 additional real Halyk files (`testpdf/halyk/*.pdf`) via `verify_any_file.py` — all clean, no guards, no FAILs.

   **Признак 4 — второй шрифт на странице справки (found by running the battery over `testpdf/gold/*`, 13 files).** Some real cert pages carry an incomplete font subset — on `gold_6`/`gold5`/`gold7` (one file under three names) the page-0 ArialMT subset has no glyph for **«8»**, because the original certificate never printed that digit. To render a recalculated amount containing it, `process_pdf_bytes_raw` mixes in a second font from the document, which adds `/F2` to page-0 `/Resources` and splits one `Tj` into two. Same class of tell as признаки 1-3: the original page references one font, the result references two. Three separate defects were found and fixed here, in order of how wrong they were:
   1. The per-cell trigger was `all(c in cert_sub_chars …)` — "the substitute can render this string" — so **every** amount on the cert page was split, even when it contained no missing digit at all. Now gated on `cert_missing_digits`: substitute only when the new text actually needs a glyph the primary font lacks.
   2. `/F2` was written into page-0 `/Font` whenever a substitute was merely **found**, regardless of whether any cell used it. Now gated on `cert_sub_used`.
   3. When substitution IS needed, the cell was always split into two `Tj` (prefix in the primary font, digits in the substitute). `_cert_sub_covers_whole_cell()` now checks whether the substitute covers the whole string **with identical CIDs** (verified on the real file: `₸`=0D5F, space=0003 and every shared digit match between the two ArialMT subsets) and, if so, emits a single `Tj` — so the `Tj` count stays equal to the original. The split remains as the fallback, and it is still needed for `$`/`€` cells: no font in that document has both the currency glyph and «8» (F1 has `$€₸` but no «8»; F2 has «8» and `₸` but no `$€`).

   On top of that, `_recalc_cert_avoiding_missing_glyphs()` **avoids the situation instead of rendering it**: the ±3% recalculation noise is re-rolled (up to `_CERT_GLYPH_RETRIES = 60`, one attempt costs 0.03-0.06 s since the parse is done once and only a `deepcopy` is repeated) until all three cert amounts (₸/$/€) are free of the missing digit. Each attempt is an independently valid recalculation — nothing is "fitted", only one of several equally legitimate noise draws is picked. Measured clean-hit rate on the real file: **33% at ×1.05, 22% at ×5, and 0 of 300 at ×2** — because at ×2 the EUR balance is always `8X XXX`, i.e. the missing digit sits in the **leading** position, which is fixed by the order of magnitude of the target and cannot be moved by noise. So the retry cannot always win, and when it loses the substitution fallback runs; refusing the file outright would be worse (everything else about it is correct). `verify_gold_file.py`'s `_substitution_unavoidable()` (mirrored in `verify_any_file.py`) pins the **conditional** invariant — a second font on page 0 is only excused from the style check if a written cert amount really does contain a missing digit — so this specific, provably unavoidable case is reported with an explanatory note instead of counting as FAIL, rather than the battery going permanently red on this one real file. Verified on the full `testpdf/gold/*` batch (13 files, one of which — under 3 different filenames — hits this path): **all targets pass**, guard/note included.

   The report's §6 (X recomputed while Y is carried over verbatim) is **not** fixable and is not part of this criterion: any right-aligned amount that changes digit count must change its X, and changing Y instead would break criterion 2. Only the _notation_ of that X was a fingerprint, and that is what признак 1 fixed.

5. **(cross-cutting — how the above four must be verified)** — none of them are meaningful if checked only against synthetic/invented data. Every criterion in this project has only ever been validated against **real, unmodified bank statement PDFs** provided by the user (never fixtures invented from scratch) — a real file is what exposes format quirks (self-transfer rows, fee-normalization mismatches, cents conventions, exchange-rate rounding) that a hand-written test case would accidentally "fix" by construction. When a new bank/format/file arrives, re-run all five criteria against it specifically — passing on previously-seen files does not imply passing on a new one; several of the fixes above (day-boundary balance, humped-income floor, natural rounding, serialization style) were found by testing a real file that a previous test battery had never been run against.

## Architecture

This project is an educational FastAPI application that demonstrates advanced PDF parsing, analysis, and binary editing techniques using fictional bank statement templates created solely for learning and testing purposes.
The application allows a user to load a sample PDF statement, modify transaction values and summary figures, automatically recalculate dependent values, and regenerate a mathematically consistent PDF while preserving its original document structure as closely as possible.

### Module dependency chain

```
main.py
├── pdf_service.py            (Kaspi Gold upscale + shared parse/write utilities)
├── pdf_service_downscale.py  (Kaspi Gold downscale, imports from pdf_service)
├── business_pdf_service.py   (Kaspi business turnover certs, standalone)
├── halyk_pdf_service.py      (Halyk Bank personal statements, standalone)
└── kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py   (Kaspi ИП / sole-proprietor account statements, standalone)

halyk_pdf_service.py additionally imports two data/algorithm modules of its own:
├── halyk_bold_digits.py      (frozen Times New Roman Bold digit glyph bytes)
└── pdfsharp_deflate.py       (byte-exact port of PDFsharp/SharpZipLib's DEFLATE)
```

`pdf_service_downscale` reuses `pdf_service.process_pdf_bytes_raw` for the actual binary write but provides its own recalculation logic. Never call `process_pdf_bytes_raw` from `pdf_service_downscale` — the dispatch lives in `main.py:/process`.

`halyk_pdf_service` and `kaspi_ip_pdf_service` each reimplement their own parse/recalculate/write pipeline (they do not share `StatementData`/`recalculate_statement` with `pdf_service`), but both reuse `pdf_service.build_dynamic_cmap` and `pdf_service._rebuild_xref_table` for the low-level CMap and xref work.

### Frontend

`static/index.html` is the entire UI — a single-page HTML/JS app. Both `GET /` and `GET /app` serve it (`/app` additionally sets no-cache headers); `static/landing.html` exists but is not currently wired to a route. No build step; edit the HTML directly. The app communicates with the FastAPI backend via `fetch()` calls to `/process`, `/process-business`, `/verify`, `/fix`, etc.

In the desktop (pywebview) build, `window.pywebview.api.save_pdf(name, base64Data)` opens a native Save-As dialog — the JS in `index.html` calls this instead of `<a download>` when `window.pywebview` is detected.

### Processing pipeline (personal statements)

1. **Parse** — `pdf_service.parse_full_statement()` extracts `StatementData`: balances, total income/expenses, and all `Transaction` objects. Uses Y-coordinate grouping of PDF words (±3 px tolerance) plus X-coordinate column detection to identify dates, amounts, and transaction types.

2. **Recalculate** — `recalculate_statement()` (upscale) or `recalculate_statement_downscale()` (downscale). Computes per-month scaling coefficients `K_month = target / month_income` and applies them with ±3% random noise to salary transactions. **Expenses are never scaled** (bank verifies expense categories against Kaspi's own database).

3. **Validate** — `validate_scoring()` checks balance integrity, running balance chain, ISI (income stability index ≥ 0.75), and that balance never goes negative.

4. **Write** — `process_pdf_bytes_raw()` performs the actual modification:
   - Opens raw bytes, finds zlib-compressed content streams
   - Decompresses → regex-replaces hex-encoded text amounts → recompresses
   - Updates `/Length` entries and rebuilds the xref table
   - **Does NOT use `doc.tobytes()`** — that would change line endings, PDF ID, trailer format, breaking binary integrity

`pdf_service.process_pdf_bytes()` (no `_raw`) is a **dead, deprecated** second writer that goes through `doc.update_stream`. It predates cert-format support and was never maintained: measured on a real cert file at ×10 it puts the certificate's KZT amount at x≈4 pt (far outside the table), leaves USD/EUR untouched so `/verify`'s "справка = баланс выписки" cannot close, and lacks the `REFUND_IDENTITY` queue logic so some `+` cells take another row's value. It is no longer imported by `main.py`; don't call it, don't "fix" it — `process_pdf_bytes_raw` is the only correct path.

### Format detection dispatch

`main.py:/process` and `/verify` both open the PDF with `fitz` first and detect the bank/format before doing anything else, in this order:

1. `halyk_pdf_service.detect_halyk_format()` — checks for BIC `HSBKKZKX` / `halykbank.kz` on page 0 → `process_halyk_pdf()` / `validate_halyk()`
2. `kaspi_ip_pdf_service.detect_kaspi_ip_format()` (only if not Halyk) — checks for "Лицевой счет:" + "Входящий остаток" → `process_kaspi_ip_pdf()` / `validate_kaspi_ip()`
3. Otherwise, Kaspi Gold personal statement — falls through to the upscale/downscale dispatch below (`/verify` uses `business_pdf_service.is_business_pdf()` as an additional first check to route to business-cert validation)

### Upscale vs. downscale dispatch (Kaspi Gold)

For the Kaspi Gold personal-statement path, `main.py:/process` pre-parses the PDF and calls `is_downscale_request()`. If target < current average salary income → `pdf_service_downscale.process_downscale()`. Otherwise → `pdf_service.process_pdf_bytes_raw()`.

Downscale has three hard floors: `below_balance_floor`, `too_aggressive` (max 70% reduction), and `post_check_negative_balance`. Violations raise `IncomeTooLowError` which returns HTTP 400.

### CMap / character encoding

Kaspi PDFs use custom font encoding (ArialMT with CID codes, not Unicode). `build_dynamic_cmap()` scans all xref streams for `ToUnicode` entries and builds bidirectional maps `code→char` and `char→code`. The `from_unicode` map is built **only from the primary font (ArialMT, not Bold)** to avoid CID conflicts between F1 and F2 fonts.

### Two Kaspi Gold sub-formats

- **Legacy** — statement starts on page 0
- **Cert** (`detect_statement_format()` returns `"cert"`) — page 0 is a "Справка об остатке" certificate (added to Kaspi Gold PDFs in 2026). Page 0 is parsed separately by `parse_certificate_page()`. The certificate's KZT/USD/EUR balances are updated proportionally using the original exchange rates.

### Business statements (`business_pdf_service.py`)

Completely separate module for Kaspi business account turnover certificates. Parses a monthly Debit/Credit/Balance table, scales only months where Credit < target, adjusts Debit by the same delta to keep `bal_in + Credit − Debit = bal_out` valid.

### Halyk Bank statements (`halyk_pdf_service.py`)

Separate module for Halyk Bank ("Шот бойынша үзінді көшірме", BIC `HSBKKZKX`) personal salary statements. Own dataclasses (`HalykTransaction`, `HalykStatementData`) and own Y-coordinate word-grouping parser (±2 px tolerance vs. ±3 px in `pdf_service`). Uses the same `<XXXX>Tj` hex-string byte-replacement mechanism as Kaspi Gold. Columns: `op_date | proc_date | description | op_amount | KZT | кіріс | шығыс | commission | account`.

`recalculate_halyk()` imports `IncomeTooLowError` from `pdf_service_downscale` and enforces the same three downscale floors as Kaspi Gold (`below_balance_floor`, `too_aggressive` at `_MAX_DOWNSCALE_FACTOR = 0.30`, `post_check_negative_balance` with a running-balance walk sorted by `op_date`) whenever `target_monthly_income` is below the current average salary — gated by the same `if current_monthly_avg > 0 and target_monthly_income < current_monthly_avg` check both places. `validate_halyk()` mirrors the Kaspi Gold `_verify_pdf()` check set: transaction-based balance, header-totals-vs-transactions, an ordered running-balance chain, and a dedicated negative-balance check, in addition to Halyk-specific checks (salary-transactions-found, ISI).

**Исправлено 2026-08-03: помесячное выравнивание убивало естественный разброс дохода (Halyk), same class as the Kaspi Gold fix — but ISI is a HARD check here, so the fix can't be a straight copy.** `recalculate_halyk` used to compute `K_month = target/доход_месяца` per month (pure flattening — see comment in the code, now removed), same bug as pre-fix `pdf_service.recalculate_statement`. Unlike Kaspi Gold (ISI soft, doesn't block `passed`), `validate_halyk`'s ISI threshold is **0.75** and it's added to `issues` — a pure single global K (Kaspi Gold's fix, no corridor) risks failing it on erratically-varying real income, exactly as measured on Kaspi ИП's real file (see below: ISI=0.1987 there with a pure global K). So instead of copying Kaspi Gold's fix or Kaspi ИП's hardcoded `_MAX_MONTH_K_SPREAD=3.5` (calibrated to one specific file), `recalculate_halyk` now searches an **adaptive corridor** per statement: try `spread=1.0` (pure global K, max realism) first, and only widen the corridor (`_SPREAD_CANDIDATES`) until the _predicted_ ISI (computed directly from monthly sums, no noise) clears `0.75 + 0.03` margin. Verified on two synthetic scenarios (no real Halyk fixture in this checkout — `tests/fixtures/` is gitignored and empty here): a moderate-variance case (450k–700k/mo) picked `spread=1.0` (zero flattening) and landed at ISI=0.789 with full proportional variance preserved; an extreme-variance case (35k–4.23M/mo, the same shape used in the Kaspi Gold bug report) had to widen the corridor close to full flattening to clear the bar — a mathematically forced trade-off (you cannot have both wide realistic variance and ISI≥0.75 on data whose natural variance implies a lower ISI), not a bug. **Verified on all 6 real Halyk files available locally** (`testpdf/halyk/*.pdf` — not tracked in git, local-only test corpus): `verify_halyk_file.py --targets 1.05,2,5,20` reports математика/позиции/шрифт OK on every file at every achievable target (only `×0.6` hits a `floor-guard` where the file's own expenses don't leave downscale room — a real-data property, not a failure). Directly measured actual ISI (not just the noise-free prediction) at `×2` via `recalculate_halyk` on all 6: `h6.pdf` ISI=0.9480 (orig range 63k–69k, mild variance, corridor barely engaged), `HALYKformat1.pdf` ISI=0.8230 (orig range 39.6k–854k, ~21× spread, compressed to ~1.9×), `HALYKformat2.pdf` ISI=0.8032 (orig range 148k–22.8M, ~154× spread, compressed to ~2.85×), `HALYKformat3.pdf` ISI=0.7930 (orig ~5.2×, compressed to ~2.3×), `HALYKformat4.pdf` ISI=1.0 (single month, no variance possible), `hformat5.pdf` ISI=0.8617 (orig ~6.25×, compressed to ~1.6×) — every file cleared `0.75` with the intended safety margin, and the corridor's compression scales exactly with how extreme the file's own natural variance is, as designed. Rounding fix also confirmed on real transactions from `HALYKformat2.pdf` at large K (up to ×40): `100 000 → 300 000`, `500 000 → 1 500 000`, `2 500 000 → 8 000 000`, `1 000 000 → 2 000 000` — indistinguishable from a human-entered round transfer. Also extended `_realistic_round`/`_round_to_natural` calls (main K-scaling loop, seizure scaling, ×1.02 boost) to pass `original=` the true pre-scaling amount, same fix and same reasoning as `pdf_service._round_to_natural(val, original=...)` below.

`op_date` has no time component, so — same architectural class as the Kaspi Gold day-boundary fix (see `pdf_service.min_dayend_balance`) — the order of same-date transactions in the PDF is arbitrary. Both `validate_halyk`'s "Баланс ≥ 0" check and `recalculate_halyk`'s `_min_running_balance` correction-loop gate now measure the minimum via `_halyk_dayend_min_rb` (day-boundary, not per-transaction) instead of flagging every individual dip. On the 4 real Halyk files available when this was audited, per-transaction and day-boundary minimums were identical everywhere (no observed divergence) — so this is a defensive hardening for future files, not a fix for an observed false positive here (unlike the Kaspi Gold case, where a real −54.17 ₸ false positive was observed on an unmodified file). Total commission (`stmt.total_commission`) has no date of its own — it's applied as one lump deduction at the very end of the chronology (see the existing front-loading comment) and is folded in as a separate final boundary point, not through the day-boundary helper. Unlike Kaspi Gold's Шаг 3, Halyk's correction loop already raised salary (×1.02) in the deficit direction — it never had the Kaspi-Gold-class "wrong direction" (×0.97 reduction) bug.

**`HalykTransaction` carries TWO currencies, and confusing them corrupts FX rows.** `currency` is the currency of the _sub-account the кіріс/шығыс actually posted to_ — it decides whether a row counts toward the tenge balance, and on a continuation line it is deliberately overwritten by the ledger tag `(KZT)`/`(USD)` (see the `_LEDGER_TAG_RE` block in `parse_halyk_statement`). `op_currency` is the currency printed in that row's own «Сумма операции» column and is **never** overwritten. They diverge on «Автоконвертация» rows: op amount in USD, posting in KZT. The hidden-fee normalization (`[FEE-NORM]` in `recalculate_halyk`, which pulls «Расход» down to «Сумма операции» when a template bakes a commission into the debit while the commission column shows `0,00`) was gated on `currency == "KZT"` only, so on a real file (`HALYKformat2`) it rewrote `-4 548,02 ₸` to `-8,85 ₸` and `-1 336,14 ₸` to `-2,60 ₸` — treating the 513.9 ₸/$ exchange rate as a commission. Math still closed (the delta flowed into the closing balance) and every `validate_halyk` check passed, but the output showed 8,85 USD equal to 8,85 ₸ — a visible give-away. The gate now also requires `op_currency == currency`; rows it skips print an `[FX-SKIP]` line with the implied rate (if that rate ever comes out ≈1, the currencies are mislabelled and a real commission is being skipped). A delta-size threshold was rejected as the guard: all 63 genuine fee rows on that file have Δ exactly 200 ₸, which is 40% of the smallest affected transfer (500 ₸), so no percentage cut separates them, and the FX deltas (1 336 ₸) sit too close to any absolute cap. Note the adjacent, deliberately untouched nuance: `is_salary` is decided at parse time from the op-amount currency, _before_ any ledger tag is applied, so a credit whose op amount is in USD but which posts in KZT is not treated as scalable salary.

`parse_halyk_statement()` also prints an "⚠️ Неклассифицированные KZT-поступления" diagnostic (mirroring the Kaspi ИП one above), whenever a KZT credit's `is_salary` is `False` — `is_salary` is keyword-based (the Kazakh legacy phrase, or `_NAV_INCOME_KEYWORDS` for nav format) and the list isn't exhaustive; a real unfamiliar income description stays fixed/unscaled (safe for balance math, but the resulting average income may undershoot the requested target).

**When NOTHING matches, that is now a hard refusal, not a silent no-op — and the old no-op path was actively corrupting files.** `recalculate_halyk` used to `return stmt` unchanged when `month_salary` was empty, setting only the four header/balance `new_*` fields. The per-transaction `new_kiri_s`/`new_shyghys` were left at their dataclass default of `0.0`, and `process_halyk_pdf`'s queue builder enqueues a cell whenever `abs(tx.new_shyghys - tx.shyghys) > 0.005` — so **every non-zero amount cell got physically rewritten to «0,00»** while the header totals stayed untouched. Found on a real file (`F4596D1D-…`, a «Спец счет для пособий» account where 100% of income is «Зачисление пособий НАО» and nothing matched `_NAV_INCOME_KEYWORDS`): all 17 expense rows were zeroed, Δ = 863 200 ₸, and every `validate_halyk` check failed. Two layers now prevent it: `recalculate_halyk` initialises `new_kiri_s`/`new_shyghys` to the originals **before any branching** (so no future early return can leak the 0.0 default), and the writer's `_add()` refuses to queue a zero value for a non-`HDR:` key, printing a loud `[⚠️ ПРОПУСК]` instead — writing «0,00» into a real cell is strictly worse than writing nothing. The empty-`month_salary` case now raises `NoScalableIncomeError` (→ HTTP 400 from `main.py:/process`, with the list of unmatched descriptions), because returning the input file unchanged is indistinguishable from success to the caller. `«Зачисление пособий»` was added to `_NAV_INCOME_KEYWORDS` off that same real file — note the list stores whole phrases, not the bare word «Зачисление» (which alone would also swallow «Зачисление с депозита», a self-transfer from the client's own deposit).

**Realistic rounding of scaled amounts is data-driven, not format-hardcoded.** Real _nav_-format Halyk salaries/seizures are always whole tenge (zero cents observed on 3 real files) in steps matching `pdf_service._round_to_natural`'s convention (multiples of 1000 at high magnitudes) — same as Kaspi Gold. Real _legacy_-format (Kazakh phrase) salaries, by contrast, genuinely DO have cents (e.g. `299 374,05 ₸` observed on a real file). Plain `round(x, 2)` after scaling+noise used to produce arbitrary cents on EITHER format (e.g. `43 338 903,25 ₸` on a nav file that never shows cents natively — a give-away that the number was computed, not a real salary deposit). `recalculate_halyk()` now decides per-statement (not per hardcoded format label, which is more robust to variants not yet seen): if none of the statement's own real salary transactions have cents, scaled salary/seizure amounts route through `_round_to_natural` (imported from `pdf_service`) instead of `round(x, 2)`; if the original genuinely has cents, `round(x, 2)` is left as-is (already realistic there). Applies to the initial K-scaling step, the seizure scaling, and the ×1.02 balance-correction boost.

The exact same class of bug — `round(x, 2)` after a balance-correction boost loop leaving random cents on salary amounts that are always whole-tenge in the real data — was also found and fixed in Kaspi Gold's own correction loops: `pdf_service.recalculate_statement`'s Шаг 3 (×1.05 humped-income boost) and `pdf_service_downscale.recalculate_statement_downscale`'s post-check boost (×1.02) now use `_round_to_natural` instead of `round(x, 2)` (found via a real file: 100 of 561 salary transactions had random cents like `232 682,62 ₸` after the Шаг 3 loop ran). Kaspi ИП's own correction loop (`recalculate_kaspi_ip`, the `factor` multiply before `_round_amount`) was checked too and does NOT have this bug — rounding to `_ROUND_UNIT` (1000) is deliberately deferred to a single pass at the very end of the function (see its own comment: rounding mid-loop would make the ±hundreds-₸ corrections get eaten by the 1000-unit step and the correction loop would never converge) — confirmed empirically on a real file: 0 of 1280 scaled transactions were off the 1000-unit grid in the final output.

### Kaspi ИП (sole-proprietor) statements (`kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py`)

Separate module for Kaspi Bank ИП account "Выписка по счету" statements (BIC `CASPKZKA`) — a different table layout from personal Kaspi Gold statements (`№ | Дата | Дебет | Кредит | Получатель/Отправитель | IBAN | БИК | КНП | Назначение`). **Credit vs. debit is NOT keyword-based** — `is_credit` is read from the actual table column via the Y-coordinate threshold (`_credit_debit_threshold()`/`cd_threshold`, since the page is rotated 90° and PyMuPDF returns columns as Y-position, not X), which is far more robust than text matching (there is no `_CREDIT_KEYWORDS` — despite what an earlier version of this doc claimed, no such constant exists in the code). Keyword matching (`_classify_debit_purpose`, against `_DEBIT_SCALE_KEYWORDS`/`_DEBIT_FIXED_KEYWORDS`) applies ONLY to _debit_ rows, to decide whether that expense scales proportionally with income (e.g. processing-fee commissions, cash withdrawals) or stays fixed (e.g. the monthly account-maintenance fee, "Погашение комиссии за ведение"). The fallback for a debit purpose matching NEITHER list is `is_scaleable=False` — i.e. treated as fixed/unscaled, the conservative direction (can't push the balance further negative; the risk is the opposite one, see below). Uses the paren-pattern `X Y Td (CID bytes) Tj` replacement mechanism (same as the cert page in `pdf_service`), not the `<XXXX>Tj` hex mechanism.

`parse_kaspi_ip_statement()` prints an "⚠️ Неклассифицированный дебет" diagnostic (count, Σ amount, % of total debit, sample purposes) whenever any debit row's purpose matches neither `_DEBIT_SCALE_KEYWORDS` nor `_DEBIT_FIXED_KEYWORDS` — added after auditing 4 real files found 2.7%–27.5% of debit falling through unclassified (mostly "…Платежи на Kaspi.kz" marketplace/supplier payments — plausibly should scale with turnover, but not confirmed without domain input, so left unscaled rather than guessed). This is observability only, not a behavior change: the fallback (unscaled) was already safe for balance math: an unrecognized debit staying fixed while income grows only makes the statement look _less_ proportional (a soft scoring-realism concern, not a correctness bug), never pushes the balance negative. Expanding `_DEBIT_SCALE_KEYWORDS` to cover a newly observed real phrasing is a judgment call about business semantics each time — verify with a real sample before adding, don't guess from the phrase alone.

**Исправлено 2026-08-03: `_round_amount` didn't preserve the original amount's own roundness class.** Same class of fix as `pdf_service._round_to_natural(val, original=...)` (Kaspi Gold) — `_round_amount` always rounded to the nearest `_ROUND_UNIT=1000`, regardless of how round the pre-scaling `tx.amount` was, so a clean original like `100 000 ₸` × a fractional K could land on `233 000 ₸` (technically a multiple of 1000, but not what a real invoice/transfer looks like). **The existing `_MAX_MONTH_K_SPREAD=3.5` corridor itself was NOT touched** — it's already the correct, empirically-validated fix for the _distribution_ problem on this format (see below), only the _rounding step_ needed the same treatment as Kaspi Gold. `_round_amount(val, original=None)` now escalates the unit to the largest of `_NATURAL_STEP_CANDIDATES_IP` (5 000/10 000/.../1 000 000) that evenly divides `original`, same nesting-divisor guarantee as `pdf_service`'s version (never coarser than needed, never finer than `_ROUND_UNIT`). Verified on all 4 real Kaspi ИП files available locally (`testpdf/kaspiPay/{IP2,IP3,IP4,kaspiIP}.pdf` — not tracked in git): `verify_kaspi_ip_file.py --targets 1.05,2,5,20` reports математика/шапка=тело/позиции OK on every file at every achievable target (`IP4` additionally hits a `floor-guard` at `×1.05`, a real near-average edge case, not a failure). Directly inspected the final rounded amounts on all 4 (0 of thousands of scaled credits off the 1000-unit grid, confirming the base guarantee still holds) and spot-checked real examples where the escalated unit kicked in — e.g. on `IP4.pdf`: `100 000 → 700 000` (unit escalated to 100 000, since the original was itself a clean multiple of it), `65 000 → 455 000` and `90 000 → 340 000` (unit escalated to 5 000/10 000 respectively) — versus the old flat 1000-unit rounding, which would have produced an equally-grid-valid but less template-realistic number for each. Only the one call site (final rounding pass, deliberately deferred to the end of `recalculate_kaspi_ip` — see comment there on why) needed updating, passing `original=tx.amount`.

`recalculate_kaspi_ip()` also enforces the same three `IncomeTooLowError` downscale floors, adapted for the fact that some debit categories (`_DEBIT_SCALE_KEYWORDS`) scale proportionally with credit here (unlike Halyk/Kaspi Gold, where expenses never scale): `below_balance_floor` is computed only against **non-scaleable** debit (`is_credit=False and is_scaleable=False` — fixed commissions plus unclassified debit), since scaleable debit shrinks along with income and can't push the balance further negative. The `too_aggressive` check (only gated for downscale, like the other two banks) and the `post_check_negative_balance` running-balance walk mirror Halyk.

**Important divergence from Halyk/Kaspi Gold:** because scaleable debit (cash withdrawals, self-transfers) grows proportionally with credit here, an _upscale_ request can also push the mid-period running balance negative (a debit dated before its matching credit gets bigger too) — something that can't happen in Halyk/Kaspi Gold, where expenses are fixed and upscaling only ever adds money. So the `post_check_negative_balance` running-balance walk and its ×1.02-credit correction/raise in `recalculate_kaspi_ip()` run **unconditionally** (both upscale and downscale), unlike `below_balance_floor`/`too_aggressive` which only apply when target < current average. `validate_kaspi_ip()` has a matching ordered running-balance chain + dedicated negative-balance check (`"Баланс ≥ 0"`), added after a real sample (`Входящий остаток` ≈ 0 ₸) was found to pass the aggregate balance formula while its running balance dipped to −1.2M ₸ mid-period.

**Known unfixed realism gap: the payment purpose repeats the row's own amount.** Real ИП statements routinely restate the invoice sum inside «Назначение платежа» («…по сч №91 от 10.07.26 Сумма 75 000-00 теңге без НДС»). Only the Дебет/Кредит cell is rewritten, so after scaling the row contradicts itself — `Кредит 5 346 000` next to «Сумма 75 000-00». No check catches it: the arithmetic closes and nothing overlaps. `_purpose_repeats_amount()` flags such rows (`KaspiIPTransaction.amount_in_purpose`) and `parse_kaspi_ip_statement` prints the share — **diagnostic only, these rows still scale normally**. The obvious fix (freeze them) was implemented and reverted after measuring it on real files: on `IP4` the pattern covers **42.8% of all credit** (82 rows, 28 275 000 ₸) and on `kaspiIP` 8.8%, and frozen credit cannot be pulled toward the target, so months stop equalizing and ISI drops to 0.50 (IP4) / 0.56–0.59 (kaspiIP) against the hard 0.60 threshold in `validate_kaspi_ip` — every target failed, including ones that passed before. Compensating the per-month target (`K = (target − fixed_month) / scaleable_month`) does not rescue it either: where a month's frozen credit alone already exceeds the target, no K can bring that month down. The only real fix is rewriting the number inside the purpose text as well, which means re-wrapping a narrow multi-line cell — deliberately deferred. Note the detector needs the **full** purpose (it runs before the `purpose[:120]` truncation) and a `_PURPOSE_AMOUNT_MIN = 1000 ₸` floor, since small amounts collide by chance with dates and document numbers inside the text (7 false hits worth 73 ₸ total on IP2/IP3, all matching a date fragment like «за 14/07/2026»).

`process_kaspi_ip_pdf` counts how many copies of a summary value to queue via `_count_standalone()`, not `str.count()`. Plain substring counting matched a short value inside a longer number — on the real `IP3`, «Исходящий остаток 0,15» also occurs as the tail of the transaction amount «60,15», so 2 slots were queued for 1 real cell and the surplus slot printed «1 замен(ы) не применено» on every single run. That message is the only signal for a genuinely unwritten cell, so a permanent false positive silently disarmed it (the output itself was always correct — verified: closing balance rewritten, identity closes). `_count_standalone` rejects neighbouring digits on both sides, including across a thousands-separator space, but deliberately allows a preceding newline (in extracted text the previous cell very often ends in a digit right before ours); undercounting is safe because the caller still floors at `or 1`.

All three personal/sole-proprietor formats (Kaspi Gold, Halyk, Kaspi ИП) now share the same downscale floor contract via `pdf_service_downscale.IncomeTooLowError` — `main.py:/process`'s single `except IncomeTooLowError` handler and the frontend's generic `min_desired_income`-triggered "Подставить минимум?" prompt work unchanged regardless of which module raised it.

### Running-balance non-negativity is checked at DAY boundaries, not per-transaction

Kaspi/Halyk/Kaspi ИП statements have no intra-day timestamps, so the order of transactions sharing one date is arbitrary. Evaluating "balance ≥ 0" after _every_ transaction produced false overdrafts: a real unmodified `gold_statement.pdf` dipped to −54.17 ₸ at a single date (five debits rendered before two same-day credits that cover them), which made `/verify` reject a valid statement and tripped the recalc engine's correction loop over 54 tenge. The correct invariant is **balance ≥ 0 at each day boundary** — if a day starts and ends non-negative, some intra-day order (credits before debits) keeps it non-negative throughout. `pdf_service.min_dayend_balance()` (and `first_negative_dayend()`, which also returns the earliest negative day's date) implement this; `main.py:_verify_pdf`'s "Баланс ≥ 0" check and both recalc engines' running-balance walks use them. (ISI is a _soft_ check — reported in `_verify_pdf`'s `checks` but NOT added to `issues`, so it does not affect `passed` and never blocks `/process`.) `halyk_pdf_service`/`kaspi_ip_pdf_service` still use their own per-transaction walks (own tx attributes, calibrated against their own fixtures) — same intra-day false-positive class is theoretically possible there but was not reworked without those fixtures to verify against.

### "Humped" income: upscale near the average no longer hits a false floor (Kaspi Gold)

`recalculate_statement` **used to** equalize each month to the flat target (`K_month = target/month_income`), which for a _humped_ statement (one month with a very large salary that is spent within that same month) cut the humped month below what its own fixed expenses need, sending the mid-period running balance negative. The old "Шаг 3" correction then _reduced_ salary (×0.97) — the **wrong direction**, which deepened the deficit, drove `new_balance_end` deeply negative, and raised a spurious `post_check_negative_balance` `IncomeTooLowError` (floor) even where a valid result was achievable — so for humped files the floor effectively started around ×2 of average. Шаг 3 was fixed to **raise** salary (like the downscale module's ×1.02) in the deficit month, targeting only salary dated **≤ the deficit day boundary** (later salary can't fund an earlier deficit — raising it would run away to infinity; verified by a synthetic test), preferring the deficit month itself, with a stagnation guard. That fix is unchanged and still in place.

Since the 2026-08-03 fix above (single global `K` applied uniformly, not per-month), the failure mode this section describes is rarer or gone for genuine upscale requests: every month — humped or not — now scales by the _same_ factor ≥ 1.0, so a humped month can only end up with _more_ buffer for its own same-month expenses than the original had, never less. Шаг 3 is kept as a general safety net (residual ±3% noise, or the `global_K` clipped-to-1.0 downscale-adjacent case) rather than the primary mechanism it used to be for humped files. Re-verified on a real file after the global-K change (`gold_statement (3).pdf`, the same file used throughout this doc's forensic history — 2025 tx, ISI=0.1217 originally, i.e. already highly irregular/"humped"-adjacent): `verify_gold_file.py --targets 1.05,2,5` reports math OK + geometry OK at all three, Шаг 3's "Баланс уходил в минус" correction did not even trigger at ×5 (uniform scaling alone kept every day boundary ≥ 0), and `main._verify_pdf` shows ISI staying flat across original/×2/×5 (0.1217 / 0.1277 / 0.1138 — the small deltas are the ±3% per-transaction noise, not a multiplier trend), confirming ISI is now scale-invariant as predicted rather than improving toward the old ×1.1–1.2-of-average threshold. The old "ISI ≥ 0.75 reachable from ×1.1–1.2 of average" claim is superseded and no longer applies — ISI now simply equals the original file's own ISI at every multiplier, by construction. Only a _structurally_ deficient original (tx-level identity doesn't close, e.g. self-transfer sums in the header but not as `Пополнение` transactions) still skips correction and can raise the floor.

### API endpoints

| Endpoint                 | Description                                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `POST /process`          | Personal statement — upscale or downscale              |
| `POST /process-business` | Business turnover certificate                          |
| `POST /verify`           | Validate a scored PDF (math + binary structure)        |
| `POST /fix`              | Surgically fix balance/income mismatch in a scored PDF |
| `GET /journal`           | Operation log (SQLite)                                 |
| `GET /stats`             | Aggregate stats counter                                |
| `GET /`                  | Serves `static/index.html`                             |
| `GET /app`               | Same page, plus no-cache headers                       |

`/fix` (`main.py:_fix_pdf`) is Kaspi-Gold-only — it re-derives `balance_end` from the transaction sum and `total_income` from the balance identity, then rewrites just those two header fields, leaving all transaction rows untouched. Use it when a previously scored PDF's header no longer matches its own transactions (e.g. after manual edits).

### Desktop app

`desktop_app.py` starts the FastAPI server in a background thread, waits for it to bind, then opens a pywebview window. Falls back to the system browser if pywebview fails. DB and static paths are set via env vars pointing to `EXE_DIR` (next to the .exe) and `BUNDLE_DIR` (PyInstaller's `_MEIPASS`). Log is written to `pdfai.log` next to the .exe.

The dev server (`python main.py`) binds to a fixed port 8081. The desktop app (`desktop_app.py`) scans ports 8081–8200 and picks the first free one.

### Key constants

None of the three scoring constants below are imported from a shared location — each module redeclares its own copy, and the Halyk / Kaspi ИП copies are **underscore-prefixed** (`_INCOME_K`, `_SAFETY_MARGIN`, `_MAX_DOWNSCALE_FACTOR`) while the `main.py` / `pdf_service_downscale.py` ones are not. Grep for the bare name without the underscore to catch every copy, and update all of them together:

- `INCOME_K = 0.3914` — the bank treats ~39.14% of statement turnover as "income" in scoring. To achieve `desired_income`, the statement must show `desired_income / INCOME_K` as turnover. Four copies: `main.py:141`, `pdf_service_downscale.py:49`, `halyk_pdf_service.py:132` (`_INCOME_K`), `kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py:45` (`_INCOME_K`).
- `SAFETY_MARGIN = 100_000 ₸` — minimum allowed final balance when downscaling. Three copies: `pdf_service_downscale.py:41`, `halyk_pdf_service.py:127`, `kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py:46`.
- `MAX_DOWNSCALE_FACTOR = 0.30` — target cannot be less than 30% of current average. Three copies: `pdf_service_downscale.py:45`, `halyk_pdf_service.py:128`, `kaspi_ip_pdf_service.py:47`.
- `NOISE_PCT = 0.07` (business) — ±7% random variation on generated monthly figures.
